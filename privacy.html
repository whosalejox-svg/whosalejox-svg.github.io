Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers Bits of OZ and "Emu War Uploader", a private tool the Bits of OZ channel owner uses to upload their own videos to YouTube.

Who uses the app

Only the Bits of OZ channel owner. The app is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to or accesses anyone else's Google or YouTube account.

What Google data the app uses

  • The app asks Google for one permission: upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload).
  • It uses that permission only to upload Bits of OZ videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own channel.
  • It does not read, collect or store viewers' personal information, comments, watch history or any other account data.

Storage and sharing

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, so uploads can run. It is never sent anywhere except Google.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Videos uploaded through the app are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers

If you watch Bits of OZ on YouTube or TikTok, those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. Bits of OZ does not run its own tracking, cookies or analytics on this website.
